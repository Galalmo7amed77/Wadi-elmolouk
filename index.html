About the Company

Textile manufacturing rooted in Samanoud since 1999

Wadi El Molouk for Spinning & Weaving is an Egyptian manufacturer of spun yarn, woven and technical fabrics. We work directly with business customers, from wholesalers and distributors to contractors and importers, and adapt production to agreed specifications.

  • Spinning, weaving and technical fabric production
